Payments Policy

Project and Premium payments are made by manual bank transfer. Account details are shown only after a signed-in user chooses to proceed.

Receipt Verification

Clients upload a transfer receipt and submit it for review. Payment status remains pending until an authorised Admin verifies it. A receipt upload is not proof of settlement.

Client Payments

Clients are required to pay the agreed project amount before work begins.

Platform Fee

Prove Talentbridge charges a 5% platform fee on applicable completed transactions.

Manual Payment Review

Payments are reviewed and confirmed only by an authorised Admin. Payouts and refunds are handled manually and are not initiated by the website.

Releases and Refunds

Payment releases and refunds are handled manually after the required review. A request on the website does not itself transfer money.

Disputes

Clients and professionals may submit disputes for review.
