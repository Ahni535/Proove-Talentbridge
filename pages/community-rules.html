Community Rules

These rules help maintain a professional and trustworthy marketplace.

1. Respect Everyone

Communicate respectfully with clients, professionals and staff.

2. Honest Work

Submit original work and do not misrepresent your experience.

3. No Fraud

Fraudulent payments, false claims and misleading activities are prohibited.

4. Platform Communication

Keep project communication within the platform. Do not share external contact details to bypass platform rules.

5. Meet Deadlines

Respect agreed project deadlines and request extensions when necessary.

6. Report Problems

Use the reporting and dispute systems when problems occur.

7. Follow Staff Instructions

Cooperate with authorised platform reviews and verification processes.
