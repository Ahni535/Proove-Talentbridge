Privacy Policy

Prove Talentbridge respects the privacy of its users.

Information We Collect

We may collect account details, contact information, occupation, profile information, project records and payment-related information needed to operate the platform.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to manage accounts, connect clients and professionals, support projects, prevent fraud and provide customer support.

Identity Verification

Documents submitted for identity verification are restricted to authorised review personnel and should not be displayed publicly.

Payment Receipts and Payout Details

Transfer receipts and payout account details are stored in Supabase and are restricted to the submitting account and authorised Admin users. Receipts are kept in private storage and are not public profile content.

Account Security

Users should keep their passwords confidential and report suspicious account activity.

Contact our support team for privacy-related enquiries.
